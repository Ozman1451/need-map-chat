# Need Map: launch video

**Video:** [`brag.mp4`](brag.mp4) (42 s, 1920×1080, 30 fps) · **Poster / thumbnail:** [`brag.jpg`](brag.jpg)

## Share copy

> 600 Instagram comments on 7 Pakistani fashion brands, clustered into 10 audience needs you can act on. Ask "What does my audience want?" and the answer draws itself, and every number on screen comes from Python.

## What's in it

Every comment is a customer telling you what to sell. The video turns comment bubbles into 600 dots, flies them to their real positions on the K-Means need map (`artifacts/real/comment_clusters.parquet`), ranks the top needs with their real shares, and shows the drill-down, per-brand and algorithm-comparison views from the app.

- The comment bubbles in the opening are illustrative examples written to match the need names, not quotes from the dataset. No real comment text or usernames appear in the video.
- All app footage comes from the uncaptioned demo recording; chart clips are cropped to the canvas.
